**Inspiration.** This project was inspired by the **SteelSeries ExactMouse Tool**, a free Mac utility that SteelSeries released in 2010. For years many Mac gamers used it to switch off mouse acceleration. When it stopped working reliably on macOS 27, ExactMouse Redux was written from scratch as an independent alternative. Its three modes (*Always On*, *On*, *Off*) follow the behaviour of the original. Credit for the original idea goes to SteelSeries.

**The name.** "Redux" means "brought back". The name *ExactMouse Redux* names the original tool as its inspiration, so that people looking for it can find a working alternative.
